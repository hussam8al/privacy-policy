Privacy Policy

Application: SlideGate: Block Escape
Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SlideGate: Block Escape ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, enjoyable gaming experience for users of all ages.

1. Information We Collect

SlideGate: Block Escape is designed as a standalone offline puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, address, or physical location).

The game stores local gameplay progress (e.g., current level, unlocked achievements, earned stars, and audio settings) solely on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless backed up via standard Android system backups managed by Google.

To support the development and maintenance of the game, we may display non-intrusive advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, primarily Google AdMob. These third-party services may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and advertising IDs, subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. May collect anonymous device identifiers, advertising ID (AAID), app activity, and crash logs to serve contextual or personalized ads in compliance with applicable laws.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Ads Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you choose to make in-app purchases (such as purchasing coins, booster packs, or removing advertisements), the transaction is processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

Our game is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party services through our game without your consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take appropriate steps to ensure the data is promptly removed.

5. Data Security

Because the Game does not operate private user databases or cloud accounts, your gameplay records stay locally on your device. We use standard industry practices to ensure that any network communications (such as fetching ad configurations) occur over secure HTTPS connections.
