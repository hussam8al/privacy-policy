Information Collection & Local Storage (Data Safety)

Google Play Data Safety Section Disclosure

The Application does not require user registration and operates no proprietary backend servers to store personal user data:

  • No Sensitive Personal Information (PII) Collected: We do not request, access, or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • Strictly On-Device Storage: Language and theme preferences, custom GFX profiles, automatic backups of Active.sav and UserCustom.ini, and Reflex Trainer scores are stored exclusively inside your device's private application storage (Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore) and never leave your device.
  • Android Auto-Backup Rules: System cloud/device transfer rules (data_extraction_rules.xml) include only your local Room profile database while excluding temporary backup files.
  • Advertising & Analytics Data via Official Google/Firebase SDKs: Pseudonymous, non-PII technical signals (fully encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and never sold by the developer) are processed solely by the official Google AdMob and Firebase SDKs detailed below to optimize ad delivery and app stability, strictly honoring your privacy consent choices.
Data Category (Google Play) Processed by Official Google/Firebase SDKs Purpose
Device or Other IDs Android Advertising ID (AD_ID, when consented), Firebase App Instance ID (app_instance_id), and Firebase Installation ID (FID). Ad serving, measurement, Firebase Remote Config delivery, and fraud prevention.
App Activity Screen views (screen_view), ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewarded completions), and non-PII properties (Pro status, app language). Ad placement/cadence optimization, AdMob User Metrics, and aggregated analytics.
App Info & Performance Ad load latency, ad load error/no-fill diagnostics, and ad-blocker/network availability state. Diagnostics to improve ad fill/show rates and overall reliability.
Approximate Location Coarse IP-derived country/region processed by Google AdMob & Firebase (never precise GPS). Regional regulatory consent checks (EEA/UK/CH) and geographically relevant ads.

Google AdMob, Firebase Ad Optimization & UMP Consent Mode v2

Complete disclosure of integrated official Google & Firebase SDKs
AD_ID • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID • com.android.vending.BILLING
  • Google AdMob Advertising (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads): To keep the Application free, we display advertisements (Anchored & Inline Adaptive banners, 300×250 banners, native ad cards, app-open ads, cold-launch & tool-activation interstitials, and rewarded / rewarded-interstitial ads to unlock FPS presets) powered by the official Google Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK may process your Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), coarse IP-based location, basic device diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics for ad delivery, frequency capping, and invalid-traffic prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use our services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (com.google.firebase:firebase-analytics): Integrated and linked with Google AdMob to power AdMob User Metrics and optimize ad quality and relevance. It collects pseudonymous, aggregated app telemetry including screen navigation (screen_view), ad load/fill latency and show outcomes, slot-level estimated ad revenue telemetry (ad_paid_revenue) for placement optimization, and non-PII user properties (ad-free Pro status, selected app language, and active access channel). No personally identifiable information (PII) is transmitted to Firebase. Processing is governed by Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Firebase Remote Config (com.google.firebase:firebase-config): Used to fetch lightweight, non-personal ad configuration parameters (such as safe cooldown intervals between full-screen ads, cold-launch preload timeouts, ad placement toggles, and A/B experiments) using a Firebase Installation ID (FID), allowing ad pacing and UX improvements without requiring an app update.
  • Google UMP (TCF v2.2) & Google Consent Mode v2 (com.google.android.ump:user-messaging-platform): For users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and other regulated regions, consent defaults start in a privacy-preserving denied/policy state (denied / eu_consent_policy) until explicit consent is collected through Google's certified UMP consent form. Your choices are synchronized directly with all four Google Consent Mode v2 signals (ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization). Users in regulated regions can review, update, or withdraw consent at any time via Settings → Privacy Options inside the app.
  • Google Play In-App Billing (com.android.billingclient:billing-ktx): The Application offers optional ad-free upgrades and subscriptions through Google Play Billing. All payment transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play; our Application never accesses, processes, or stores your credit card or banking information.
  • Google Play In-App Updates & Review (play:app-update-ktx & play:review-ktx): Used solely to check Google Play for mandatory version updates and to display Google Play's native in-app review sheet.

    Legacy Storage Fallback (Android 7–10 only): Used solely on API 24–29 to read or export configuration backups to the Downloads directory.

    Network & Local Wi-Fi Discovery: Used to measure connection latency (ping) locally, discover local on-device Wireless Debugging pairing services (mDNS), serve Google AdMob ads, run Firebase Analytics & Remote Config, manage UMP consent, and verify Google Play Billing purchases and mandatory updates.
